# Accessibility

The web app targets **WCAG 2.2 AA**: keyboard navigation with visible focus, skip link and landmarks, colour contrast
checked in both themes, targets of at least 24 px, reduced-motion support, charts that never rely on colour alone
(each has a data table and a text summary), math rendered with MathML, and both languages (English, Spanish).
Every page is scanned with axe in CI, in light and dark themes and in both languages.

Found a barrier? Please open an issue with the page, what you tried, and your assistive technology/browser.
