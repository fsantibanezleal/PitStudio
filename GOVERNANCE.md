# Governance

PitStudio is maintained by a single maintainer (Felipe A. Santibanez-Leal), who has final say on scope, design and
releases. Architecture decisions are recorded as decision records in
[`docs/architecture/decisions/`](docs/architecture/decisions/) (`DEC-NNNN-title.md`); requirements and their changes
live in [`specs/`](specs/). Regular contributors may be invited to become maintainers.
